# EPIC-05. Starter Packs and Reference Playbooks

**Status:** Proposed
**Milestone:** M2
**Owner:** TBD
**Last updated:** 2026-03-28

## Objective

Ship a small set of first-party packs and real operational playbooks that prove Guild is useful.

## Why now

Guild needs outcome-shaped proof, not just schema and packaging plumbing.

## In scope

- four curated starter packs
- six reference playbooks
- demo fixtures
- walkthrough docs

## Out of scope

- broad integration matrix
- community submissions
- dozens of shallow packs

## Deliverables

- `incident-triage`
- `k8s-remediation`
- `safe-change`
- `secrets-and-edge`
- six playbook specs with examples and docs

## Dependencies

- EPIC-03 and EPIC-04

## Risks

- too many toy examples
- demos focused on internals instead of outcomes
- packs without sharp use cases

## Exit criteria

- at least 4 packs and 6 playbooks exist
- each playbook includes approvals, capabilities, and evidence contract
- docs show a clear before / during / after workflow
