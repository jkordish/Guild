## Practical Use For Counsel

- The best comparison target is not "all sandboxes" or "all capability systems."
- The strongest measured distinction is the combined chain of upper-bound admission, bounded live proof, downstream proof-backed linkage, and explicit fail-closed fallback or refusal.
- The weakest place to overreach is anything phrased as runtime-general proof, broad `emit-evidence` proof, broad `invoke-skill` proof, or broad outbound HTTP proof.
